# Interview Explanation

I developed a Salesforce-based CRM system to manage customers, leads, and follow-up activities.

I created three custom objects: Customer, Lead, and Follow Up. A Lookup Relationship connects Follow Up with Customer because one customer can have multiple follow-ups.

I used validation rules and formula fields for data quality and dynamic information. I used Flow Builder for CRM automation.

On the development side, I created an Apex controller using SOQL, a Customer Trigger, and an Apex Test Class. I also built a Lightning Web Component that displays customer information and the total customer count.

Finally, I created Reports and a CRM Sales Dashboard for business visibility.
